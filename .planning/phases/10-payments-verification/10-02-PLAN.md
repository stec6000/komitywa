<task type="checkpoint:human-verify">
  <name>Task 2: Full E2E purchase flow — browse, cart, checkout, P24 sandbox payment</name>
  <what-built>
The ebook product is live, P24 sandbox credentials are configured, and the webhook URL auto-builds as https://kuchennakomitywa.pl/zamowienie/webhook/p24/ via request.build_absolute_uri() + SECURE_PROXY_SSL_HEADER.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Follow these steps exactly in a browser. Use a real email address you can check — the confirmation and ebook emails will be sent there.

**1. Browse the shop.**
Go to https://kuchennakomitywa.pl/sklep/
Confirm the ebook product from Task 1 is visible.

**2. Add to cart.**
Click the ebook product → open its detail page → click the "Dodaj do koszyka" button.
Confirm you are redirected to the cart page and the item is listed.

**3. Proceed to checkout.**
Click "Przejdź do kasy" (or equivalent checkout button).
Fill in the checkout form:
- Email: your real email address
- Name: your name
- Phone: any valid phone number
- Pickup date: any future date (required by the form, ignored for ebooks)

Click the submit button. You should be redirected to the P24 sandbox payment page.

**4. Complete payment in P24 sandbox.**
On the P24 sandbox payment page:
- Select any payment method shown
- Use P24 sandbox test card / BLIK / bank transfer (the sandbox accepts any test data)
- Complete the payment flow

After successful payment, P24 will redirect you back to https://kuchennakomitywa.pl/zamowienie/powrot/?order_id=X

**5. Check the return page.**
The return page should show order confirmation. Note the order ID shown.

**6. Verify order status in Django admin.**
Go to: https://kuchennakomitywa.pl/admin/shop/order/
Find the order by ID from step 5.
Confirm the status field shows **"paid"** (not "pending").

If the status is still "pending" after 60 seconds, the webhook did not fire. Check production logs:
```bash
ssh USERNAME@serwer.mydevil.net
tail -50 ~/domains/kuchennakomitywa.pl/public_html/logs/django.log
```
Look for P24 webhook entries or error messages.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" when order status shows "paid" in admin, or describe what went wrong</resume-signal>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:human-verify">
  <name>Task 3: Verify both emails received — order confirmation + ebook PDF attachment</name>
  <what-built>
After the webhook fires and sets order.status = "paid", the webhook view calls send_order_confirmation(order) and send_ebook_delivery(order) in sequence. send_ebook_delivery attaches the ebook PDF from product.ebook_file.path and sends it to order.email.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Check the inbox of the email address you used at checkout. Within 2 minutes of payment completion you should receive two separate emails:

**Email 1 — Order confirmation**
- Subject: "Potwierdzenie zamówienia #X — Kuchenna Komitywa"
- Body: plain text listing the ordered item(s), total, pickup date
- No PDF attachment

**Email 2 — Ebook delivery**
- Subject: "Twoje ebooki — Kuchenna Komitywa"
- Body: short message about the attachment
- Attachment: the test PDF you uploaded in Task 1 (application/pdf MIME type)

If emails are not arriving after 3 minutes:
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Check production logs on the server: `tail -100 logs/django.log` — look for "Failed to send" errors
3. Confirm DEFAULT_FROM_EMAIL is set in .env and the Brevo SMTP credentials (EMAIL_HOST_USER, EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD) are present

Both emails must arrive to consider this task complete.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" when both emails are received with correct content and the ebook PDF is attached to email 2, or describe what is missing</resume-signal>
</task>
